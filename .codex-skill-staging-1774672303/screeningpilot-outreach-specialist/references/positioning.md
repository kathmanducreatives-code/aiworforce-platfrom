# ScreeningPilot positioning

## Core message
- Eliminate external recruiting middlemen.
- Hire direct using automated screening.
- Reduce agency fees, typically in the 15-25% range.
- Improve speed to qualified candidates and time-to-hire.

## Who to target
- Founders, CEOs, and operator leaders in growth companies.
- People/HR leaders responsible for hiring outcomes.
- Tech companies and funded startups in active hiring phases.

## Messaging guardrails
- Sound like a pragmatic founder/operator, not a generic salesperson.
- Anchor on one real hiring or growth signal from the lead.
- Keep tone specific, concise, and low-hype.
- Avoid hard-sell CTAs (for example, "book a demo" or "sign up now").
