## 1. Product-Market Fit Summary

**What ScreeningPilot does:**
90% of hiring runs automatically — AI interviews every applicant, filters the unworthy, delivers ranked candidates. No agencies. No recruiting team. No manual screening.

**The core pain we solve:**
Startup founders spending 20+ hours/week reviewing resumes, scheduling screens, and interviewing bad candidates — while their product stalls.

**Why they'll eagerly buy:**
- Eliminates $15K-$40K agency fees per hire
- Saves 20+ hrs/week of founder time
- Every applicant gets interviewed (no good ones slip through)
- Platform is whitelabeled — looks like their own
- Expert Marketplace gives access to 12,000+ specialist interviewers

**CTA is always:** Book a demo OR ask to send a video walkthrough. **Never mention pricing in outreach.**

## 2. ICP Definition — The 3 Tiers

|-----------|---------|
| **Company size** | 1-15 employees |
| **Stage** | Pre-seed, Seed, Series A |
| **Funding** | Raised in last 0-30 days |

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Company size** | 15-50 employees |
| **Stage** | Series A, early Series B |
| **Hiring need** | Multiple open roles, scaling engineering team |
| **Decision maker** | VP Engineering, CTO, or Head of People (no dedicated TA team yet) |
| **Pain** | Agency costs becoming unsustainable at scale |
| **Signal** | Growing fast, posting multiple roles, funding announced 1-3 months ago |

**Conversion expectation:** 8-12% demo booking rate

| Attribute | Details |

## 3. The 5 Buyer Personas

### Persona 1: "Solo Technical Founder"
- **Profile:** Building product themselves, just raised Seed ($1-4M)
- **Team:** 1-5 people, all technical
- **Hiring need:** First 2-3 engineers. Never hired before.
- **Pain:** Agency quoted $25K per hire — feels like robbery. Spending entire weekends reviewing 200+ resumes. Good candidates ghosting because process takes too long.
- **Trigger:** Posted a job 2+ weeks ago, still looking
- **What they'll buy first:** AI Screening + Screening Portal
- **Find on:** Wellfound, YC Jobs, X/Twitter, Indie Hackers, Product Hunt launches

### Persona 2: "Non-Technical Founder"
- **Profile:** Business/sales background, has idea + funding, can't evaluate engineers
- **Team:** 1-10 people, mostly business side
- **Hiring need:** Needs technical co-founder or first 3 engineers
- **Pain:** Can't tell a good engineer from a bad one. Terrified of making a $150K/yr mistake.
- **Trigger:** Posted "looking for CTO" or "hiring first engineer"
- **What they'll buy first:** Expert Marketplace + AI Screening (the combo is magic for them)
- **Find on:** LinkedIn, startup Slack communities, YC network, bootstrapper communities

### Persona 3: "Scaling CTO"
- **Profile:** Technical leader at 10-30 person startup that just hit PMF
- **Team:** 5-15 engineers, needs to double in 6 months
- **Hiring need:** 5-10 engineers across multiple roles
- **Pain:** Agency fees at this scale = $150K-$400K. Spending 30% of time on hiring instead of architecture.
- **Trigger:** Series A announced, multiple job posts appearing
- **What they'll buy first:** Full platform — AI Screening + ICP Lookalike + Expert Marketplace
- **Find on:** Wellfound Series A filter, LinkedIn, funding announcements, HN "Who's Hiring"

### Persona 4: "Overwhelmed Head of People"
- **Profile:** First People/HR hire at a 20-50 person startup
- **Team:** Solo HR person handling EVERYTHING
- **Hiring need:** 10+ open roles, drowning in applicants
- **Pain:** One person can't screen 500 applicants/month. No budget for a full TA team yet.
- **Trigger:** "Hiring our first recruiter" posts, or "how do you screen at scale" questions
- **What they'll buy first:** Screening Portal + AI Interviews (immediate time relief)
- **Find on:** LinkedIn, HR/People Ops Slack communities, SHRM forums

### Persona 5: "The VC Portfolio Partner"
- **Profile:** Partner or Platform Lead at a Seed/Series A fund
- **Team:** Manages 20-50 portfolio companies, all hiring
- **Pain:** Portfolio companies keep asking for recruiting help. Fund reputation depends on helping companies scale.
- **Trigger:** Portfolio company just raised, fund announced new investments
- **What they value:** Looks like a value-add they can offer across the portfolio
- **Find on:** LinkedIn, VC Twitter/X, fund websites, Demo Day attendees

## 4. Lead Scoring System (0-10)

| Signal | Points | How to Detect |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| Team size under 15 (founder doing hiring) | **+3** | LinkedIn company page, Wellfound, Crunchbase |
| Seed or Series A stage | **+3** | Crunchbase, funding announcements, Seedtable |
| 1-5 open engineering roles right now | **+2** | Job boards, company careers page, Wellfound |
| No HR/recruiting team listed | **+2** | LinkedIn — check if anyone has "recruiter" or "talent" in title |
| Founder personally posted the job | **+1** | LinkedIn job post author, X/Twitter |
| YC or accelerator backed | **+1** | YC directory, accelerator portfolios |
| Remote-first company | **+1** | Job listing mentions remote, company page |
| Agency complaint signal detected | **+1** | Social media posts, comments on recruiting content |
| Funding announced in last 30 days | **+1** | Crunchbase alerts, Seedtable, TechCrunch |

### Score Tiers:

| Score | Action | Timeline |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **HOT (8-10)** | Immediate personalized outreach. Research the founder, reference their exact job post or pain signal. | Today — within hours |
| **WARM (5-7)** | Queue for outreach within 48hrs. Slightly softer CTA — offer video walkthrough. | This week |
| **COOL (3-4)** | Add to nurture list. Follow on social, engage with their content first. | Next 2 weeks |
| **SKIP (<3)** | Archive. Do not contact. | Never |

## 5. Buying Signals to Detect

### HOTTEST — Act Immediately

| Signal | Example | Where to Find |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| "We just raised" + hiring engineers | "Excited to announce our $3M seed! Now hiring our founding engineering team" | LinkedIn, X, TechCrunch |
| "Still looking for" (slow search = pain) | "2 months in and still looking for a senior backend engineer" | LinkedIn, X, Indie Hackers |
| Agency fee complaint | "Just got quoted $30K to fill one role... there has to be a better way" | X, Reddit, Slack communities |
| Overwhelmed with applications | "Got 400 applications for one role, how do you even screen these?" | LinkedIn comments, X |
| Founder personally posted the job | Job listing where the poster is CEO/Founder, not HR | LinkedIn Jobs, Wellfound |
| "Can't find good engineers" | "Why is it so hard to find engineers who can actually code?" | X, HN, Indie Hackers |
| Offering referral bounties | "$5K referral bonus for anyone who can find us a React dev" | LinkedIn, X, company blog |

### WARM — Act Within 48 Hours

| Signal | Where to Find |
|--------|---------------|
| Commenting on hiring/recruiting content | LinkedIn post comments on recruiting thought leaders |
| Multiple open engineering roles | Wellfound, LinkedIn Jobs, company careers page |
| Funding announcement (no hiring mention yet) | Crunchbase, Seedtable, TechCrunch |
| "Growing the team" posts | LinkedIn, company blog |
| Interview process frustration discussions | HN threads, Reddit r/startups, X |

### COLD — Skip

| Signal | Why Skip |
|--------|----------|
| HR team posting the job (not founder) | They have recruiting infrastructure |
| Large company (200+ employees) | Long sales cycle, procurement gates |
| Non-engineering roles only | Not our core value prop |
| No funding, no revenue signals | Can't afford tooling |
| "Head of Talent Acquisition" listed | Already have TA team |

## 6. Lead-Finding Channels (Ranked by ROI)

### Rank 1: Wellfound (Highest Quality)
- **URL:** https://wellfound.com/companies
- **Filter:** 1-10 employees → Seed/Series A → Hiring → Software
- **Why #1:** Every company is a startup, founder-led, and actively hiring
- **Volume:** 500+ matching companies at any time
- **Scrape with:** Firecrawl on `wellfound.com/startups/industry/software-engineering-3`

### Rank 2: YC Companies Directory
- **URL:** https://www.ycombinator.com/companies
- **Filter:** B2B → Hiring → 1-50 employees
- **Why:** Every YC company is pre-vetted, funded, and the founder IS the decision maker
- **Volume:** 200+ currently hiring
- **Key pages:**
  - https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/b2b/hiring
  - https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/developer-tools/hiring
  - https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/saas/hiring

### Rank 3: Funding Announcement Databases
- **Seedtable:** https://www.seedtable.com/funding-rounds/recent
- **Fundraise Insider:** https://fundraiseinsider.com/blog/seed-startups/
- **GrowthList:** https://growthlist.co/seed-startups/
- **Crunchbase:** Filter for Seed/Series A, Software, last 30 days
- **Why:** Fresh funding = immediate hiring budget = urgent need
- **Timing:** Scrape weekly, outreach within 1-3 days of announcement

### Rank 4: LinkedIn Signal Mining
- **Method 1 — Profile Search:** Find founders at 1-50 person software companies
- **Method 2 — Post Monitoring:** Track posts about hiring, recruiting pain, team growth
- **Method 3 — Comment Scraping:** Find people engaging with recruiting content creators
- **Method 4 — Job Post Scraping:** Find founder-posted engineering roles
- **Tools:** Apify LinkedIn Profile Search, Firecrawl, PhantomBuster

### Rank 5: X/Twitter Real-Time Signals
- **Search queries:**
  - `"we're hiring" AND "engineers" AND ("seed" OR "startup" OR "founding")`
  - `"can't find" AND ("engineers" OR "developers")`
  - `"recruiting agency" AND ("expensive" OR "fees" OR "cost")`
  - `"first engineer" OR "founding engineer" OR "hiring our first"`
- **Tools:** Grok search, Firecrawl on X search results

### Rank 6: Indie Hackers / Product Hunt
- **Indie Hackers:** https://www.indiehackers.com — search "hiring first employee"
- **Product Hunt:** Recent launches → Check if founders are now hiring
- **Why:** Solo founders about to scale = perfect Persona 1

### Rank 7: Hacker News "Who's Hiring"
- **URL:** Monthly "Who's Hiring" threads on HN
- **Filter:** Small team mentions, founder contact email, remote roles
- **Scrape with:** Firecrawl on the monthly thread URL

### Rank 8: Startup Slack Communities
- **Demand Curve, YC Alumni, Online Geniuses, Startup School**
- **Monitor for:** "How do you screen candidates?", "Recruiting recommendations?", "Agency alternative?"
- **Approach:** Value-first, not pitchy

## 7. Firecrawl Scraping Playbook

### Scrape 1: YC Companies Currently Hiring

```
Target URL: https://www.ycombinator.com/companies?batch=&demo_day=&industry=B2B+Software+and+Services&isHiring=true&query=&regions=United+States&stage=Seed&subindustry=&tags=&team_size=1-10
Extract: Company name, description, team size, batch, job links, founder names
Frequency: Weekly (Monday morning)
Score: Auto-score 7+ (YC + hiring + small team)
```

### Scrape 2: Wellfound Seed Startups Hiring Engineers

```
Target URL: https://wellfound.com/role/software-engineer
Filter: Seed stage, 1-10 employees, Remote OK
Extract: Company name, role title, company size, founding date, founders
Frequency: Twice weekly (Monday + Thursday)
Score: Auto-score based on team size + stage + role count
```

### Scrape 3: Seedtable Recent Funding Rounds

```
Target URL: https://www.seedtable.com/funding-rounds/recent
Filter: Seed and Series A only, Software/SaaS category
Extract: Company name, amount raised, date, investors, website URL
Frequency: Daily
Follow-up: Visit company website → check careers page → count open engineering roles
Score: Funding in last 7 days + engineering roles = HOT
```

### Scrape 4: Fundraise Insider Seed Startups

```
Target URL: https://fundraiseinsider.com/blog/seed-startups/
Extract: Company name, funding amount, date, industry, location
Filter: USA/UK/EU, Software/Tech, last 30 days
Frequency: Weekly
```

### Scrape 5: LinkedIn Job Posts by Founders

```
Target: LinkedIn job search API via Apify
Filters:
  - Job title contains: "Software Engineer" OR "Frontend" OR "Backend" OR "Full Stack"
  - Company size: 1-10, 11-50
  - Posted by: Founder, CEO, CTO (check poster title)
  - Posted in: Last 7 days
Extract: Company name, poster name + title, job title, company size
Score: If poster is Founder/CEO → +3 points automatically
Frequency: Twice weekly
```

### Scrape 6: X/Twitter Hiring Pain Signals

```
Search queries via Firecrawl:
  1. "hiring engineers" startup founder -recruiter -agency (filter: last 7 days)
  2. "can't find developers" OR "impossible to hire" (filter: last 30 days)
  3. "recruiting costs" OR "agency fees" startup (filter: last 30 days)
  4. "founding engineer" OR "first engineer" hiring (filter: last 7 days)
Extract: Username, tweet text, engagement count, profile bio
Score: If bio says "Founder" or "CEO" at <50 person company → HOT
Frequency: Daily
```

### Scrape 7: Hacker News "Who's Hiring" Thread

```
Target URL: Latest "Ask HN: Who is hiring?" thread (posted 1st of each month)
Extract: Company name, role, contact info, remote/onsite, team size mentions
Filter: Posts mentioning <20 person team, seed/startup stage, engineering roles
Score: Direct founder email + small team + engineering = 8+
Frequency: Monthly (on the 1st)
```

## 8. LinkedIn Signal Mining

### Strategy 1: Direct Profile Search (Apify)

Use Apify's `harvestapi/linkedin-profile-search` with these exact filters:

```json
{
  "searchQuery": "Founder hiring engineers",
  "currentJobTitles": ["Founder", "Co-Founder", "CEO", "CTO"],
  "companyHeadcount": ["A", "B", "C"],
  "seniorityLevelIds": ["310", "320"],
  "locations": ["United States", "United Kingdom", "Germany", "Netherlands"],
  "industryIds": ["4"],
  "functionIds": ["9"],
  "maxItems": 50,
  "profileScraperMode": "Full"
}
```

**Variations to run:**
1. `"hiring first engineer"` — catches Persona 1 & 2
2. `"building engineering team"` — catches Persona 3
3. `"looking for developers"` — broad signal
4. `"scaling team"` + Series A filter — catches Persona 3

### Strategy 2: Comment Scraping on Recruiting Content

**Target creators who post about hiring/recruiting:**
- Follow top LinkedIn voices in recruiting/startup hiring
- Scrape comments on their viral posts about hiring pain
- Filter commenters by: Founder/CEO title + company size <50
- These people are self-identifying their pain

**Example post topics to monitor:**
- "Why hiring is broken"
- "Agency fees are insane"
- "How we hired our first 10 engineers"
- "Stop paying recruiters"

### Strategy 3: LinkedIn Job Post Author Analysis

- Scrape LinkedIn Jobs for: Software Engineer + Startup + <50 employees
- Check WHO posted the job (not the company, the person)
- If the poster is a Founder/CEO → TIER 1 lead
- If the poster has "Recruiter" or "TA" in title → SKIP

### Strategy 4: "Recently Changed Jobs" Filter

```json
{
  "searchQuery": "Head of People OR Head of HR",
  "companyHeadcount": ["B", "C"],
  "recentlyChangedJobs": true,
  "maxItems": 30
}
```
**Why:** A startup that just hired their first HR person = about to start hiring at scale. They need tools.
