## The one failure, classified

**M07, phrasing "exclusion restated as hard 'excluded_roles'", repeat 3.** The expected exclusion `sales_development_representative` was missing.

- **Root cause: model output, made worse by validation.**
  - The model quoted the excluded role correctly (`span: "SDRs"`, `relation: "excluded"`), but left both `title` and `family` null.
  - The shared validator rejected the role as `no_role_id`. The user's explicit exclusion then vanished from the contract; it was visible only in `rejected`.
  - The other 5 M07 answers named the title and were correct.
- **This is a semantic defect, even though the gate passes:** a requirement the user stated was silently dropped.
- **Fixed in the v4 interpreter** (`missionContract/interpret.ts`):
  1. A quoted role without an id is first resolved from the user's own word through the reviewed title registry (`canonicalTitleOf`: "SDRs" → `sales_development_representative`).
  2. A role that still cannot be identified becomes an **approval-blocking question**, never a silent omission.
- **Regression tests:**
  - the exact recorded answer;
  - a role the registry cannot identify (it must become a question);
  - all 165 recorded answers through the current interpreter, which now read **165/165**.

  All three fail without the fix.
- No paid rerun was made. The fix is verified offline against the recorded answers.
