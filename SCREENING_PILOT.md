# Screening Pilot: The End of Recruiting Agencies

Screening Pilot is an AI-powered talent acquisition platform designed to replace traditional recruiting agencies. By automating the most expensive and time-consuming parts of the hiring funnel—sourcing, screening, and outreach—Screening Pilot allows companies to hire at scale for a flat monthly fee, saving tens of thousands in agency commissions.

## 🎯 Target Customers

- **Startups & Scale-ups**: Growing teams that need to hire quickly without the €15k–€30k per-hire agency fee.
- **Internal HR Teams**: Recruiters who want to 10x their efficiency by automating manual sourcing and initial screening.
- **Enterprise Hiring Managers**: Leads who want direct access to the best talent in the market without middlemen.
- **VCS & Foundries**: Organizations that need to spin up specialized teams for portfolio companies instantly.

## 🚀 Core Objectives

1.  **Eliminate Minimum 90% of Hiring Costs**: Replace success fees with a flat €149/mo subscription.
2.  **Instant Market Access**: Move from "searching for candidates" to "ranking the entire market."
3.  **Automated Quality Control**: Use AI to vet every applicant against your Ideal Candidate Profile (ICP) before you even see them.
4.  **Expert-Led Validation**: Bridge the gap between AI screening and human expertise through a vetted marketplace.

## 🛠 Features in Detail

### 1. Lookalike Engine (Market-Wide Mapping)
Instead of writing complex Boolean strings, you give the AI a "seed" (an ideal candidate's LinkedIn profile).
- **Trajectory Analysis**: The engine analyzes career progression, skill velocity, industry pivots, and seniority levels.
- **Deep Search**: It scans the entire LinkedIn ecosystem to find every professional with a similar profile.
- **15-Minute Sourcing**: Move from one seed profile to 2,000+ ranked matches in under 15 minutes.

### 2. AI Job Screening & Adaptive Portals
Create custom screening landing pages that act as your first-round AI interviewer.
- **Custom ICP Matching**: Define roles with granular requirements (Experience, Education, Skills, Salary).
- **Shareable Links**: Generate unique URLs to post on job boards or send directly to leads.
- **Scoring Engine**: Candidates are automatically ranked (1–10) with percentile comparisons against the entire talent pool.

### 3. Expert Marketplace (Human-in-the-Loop)
When you need deep technical validation, leverage our network of 12,000+ vetted industry experts.
- **Verified Professionals**: Access the top 1% of specialists in AI, Legal, Finance, DevOps, and more.
- **Technical Scorecards**: Experts conduct rigorous evaluations of candidate skills (System Design, Distributed Systems, Problem Solving).
- **Interview Hub**: View saved recordings, AI-analyzed transcripts, and confidence scores from expert interviews.
- **Final Vetted Ratings**: Candidates receive a final rating (up to 5.0) from domain experts, ensuring zero-guesswork hiring.



## 📝 Step-by-Step Usage Guide

### Step 1: Define Your Target (The "Seed")
Find a LinkedIn profile of someone who would be a perfect hire. Paste their URL into the **Lookalike Engine** to map the entire addressable market.

### Step 2: Configure the Screening Role
Go to **Create New Screening** and enter:
- **Role Specs**: Job Title, Company, and Requirements.
- **Generate Link**: Get a unique URL for your screening portal.

### Step 3: Source & Score
Let the AI find and score candidates. Within 15 minutes, your dashboard will populate with ranked matches sorted by Fit Score.

### Step 4: Validate via Expert Marketplace
For top candidates, request an expert interview.
- **Select Specialist**: Choose a verified expert from the marketplace.
- **Review Scorecard**: Analyze the 90+ point technical scorecard and behavioral analysis generated by the expert.
- **Watch Hub**: Review interview recordings and transcripts in the **Interview Hub**.

### Step 5: Activate Outreach Loop
Select your finalists and trigger the **Outreach Engine** or the **Power Dialer**.
- The system will dial or email through the list, handling all follow-ups.
- You only step in when a candidate hits your "Interview Scheduled" or "Hired" milestone.

## ⚙️ Technical Architecture
- **Frontend**: Vite + React + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion/GSAP.
- **Workflow Engine**: **n8n** hosting automated loops for dialing and sourcing.
- **Telephony**: **Twilio** integration with AMD for high-efficiency cold calling.
- **Intelligence**: Adaptive AI scoring engines and career trajectory analysis models.
- **Data Layer**: Supabase for real-time candidate management and interaction history.
